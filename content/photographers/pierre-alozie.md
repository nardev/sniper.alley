Pierre Alozie was born in Lagos in 1961 and grew up between Nigeria, Sweden, England, France and Kenya. The Biafran war, which sent his family back to France while his father, a doctor, stayed on, was an early lesson in how history reaches ordinary lives. After studying chemical engineering at Syracuse University, he moved to Paris in 1984 and discovered photography. To understand the culture behind the craft, he started in the theatre, photographing productions and portraits of actors in Paris and, later, at festivals across Italy. Theatre taught him culture, composition and the breadth of human expression. In 1985, while photographing a student demonstration in Paris, he recognised those same expressions on the street, and the link between politics and the arts became clear to him. That connection took him to Berlin in 1988, where he published his first photographs in Die Tageszeitung. In 1989 he covered the fall of the Wall, the Velvet Revolution in Prague, and events in Timisoara, Bulgaria and Kosovo. In 1992 he documented the Siege of Sarajevo. His work led to collaborations with European newspapers including The Independent and Trouw, and to exhibitions in Germany, the USA and London. His 288-page book Europe in Flux: Peering Through an Outsider-Insider Lens (2019) was exhibited at the 12 Star Gallery, European Parliament Office, London. Now based in London, he is working on a long-term project, provisionally titled Brexit, documenting five years of Brexit in the UK. In over 32 years as a photographer, Alozie has watched conditions for freelancers, including himself, deteriorate. He worked with the Frontline Freelance Register, the NUJ, the ACOS Alliance and others to bring diversity and safer, fairer working conditions to the profession. With the freelance media under sustained attack, Pierre considers it vital to support young photographers documenting these difficult times, as well as to help established professionals whose incomes have shrunk since the rise of internet publishing, while exploring the opportunities new digital platforms offer.
