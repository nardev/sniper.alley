In this episode of our ‘The Story Behind the Photo’ series, Italian photojournalist Enrico Dagnino tells us the background story of one of his most memorable photos taken in Bosnia, during his war coverage in Sarajevo in late April 1992.

He explains how he managed to snap the photographs of the execution of innocent civilians while being arrested by Arkan soldiers in Ilidza.
