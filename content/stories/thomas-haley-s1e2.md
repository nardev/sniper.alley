In the second episode of our ‘The Story Behind the Photo’ series, American photojournalist Thomas Haley tells us the background story of one of his photos taken in Sarajevo during September 1992.
Thomas describes how he covered the war as a photojournalist, his approach and main focus during the Siege of Sarajevo.
