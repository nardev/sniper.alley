Our 5th episode of ‘The Story Behind The Photo’ series with the German photojournalist Peter Kullmann is about 'Sarajevo Safari' and 'weekend chetniks' who used to come for a weekend from Germany in order to shoot and kill innocent people in Sarajevo Under The Siege. Peter talks about Serb snipers and their positions around the city. Highly emotional episode.
