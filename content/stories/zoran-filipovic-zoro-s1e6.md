In the 6th episode of ‘The Story Behind The Photo’ series Sniper Alley Photo brings you Zoran Filipovic Zoro and his wok from Sarajevo.  Zoran describes his experience, how he came to the city and talks about his favorite photo he made during the Siege of the capital of Bosnia and Herzegovina. This episode is a digital exhibition of stunning and powerful images Zoran shared with us.
