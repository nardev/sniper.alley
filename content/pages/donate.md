<p class="kicker">English</p>

If you value our work and our mission of preserving Sarajevo's visual wartime record and would like to help us continue working on it, please consider sponsoring us.
Your donation helps cover the time and costs involved in creating and maintaining this digital gallery. We are in need to buy video, audio and computer equipment in order to make new films and new episodes of The Story Behind The Photo mini-docu series. Additionaly each episode also costs money and time, you can help us cover production and post production expenses for this valuable oral history documentation. These stories and images matter to all of us. A donation is one of the simplest ways to help sustain our Sniper Alley Photo project and to keep the Sarajevo Siege photo archive alive and growing. Your support, every contribution, big or small, makes a real difference.

You can support us through our PayPal account.

Ako cijenite naš rad i našu misiju očuvanja vizuelnog ratnog zapisa Sarajeva pod opsadom, te želite pomoći da se naš rad nastavi, razmislite o tome da nas sponzorišete.
Vaša donacija pomaže u pokrivanju troškova stvaranja i održavanja ove digitalne galerije. Potrebna nam je video, audio i računarska oprema kako bismo snimili nove filmove i nove epizode mini-dokumentarne serije "Priča iza fotografije". Pored toga, svaka epizoda takođe košta novca i vremena, a vi nam možete pomoći da pokrijemo troškove produkcije i postprodukcije za ovu vrijednu dokumentaciju usmene historije. Ove priče i slike važne su svima nama. Vaša donacija jedan je od najjednostavnijih načina da pomognete u održavanju našeg projekta Sniper Alley Photo i da pomognete da arhiva fotografija opsade Sarajeva nastavi rasti i razvijati se. Vaša podrška, svaka vrsta doprinosa, velika ili mala, čini ogromnu razliku.
