~ 
My brother was born in 1979.  
 
I can’t but not notice that year whenever and wherever I see it.  
 
Especially when I hear it.  
 
Every time I meet people and we get into conversation about age,  
I tell them mine and they mention theirs, I quickly do the math in my head.  

Trying to see if they fall under that dear category of people.  
 
If they meet the criteria to be treated with special care.  
 
Nineteen seventy-nine.  
 
I stop.  
 
Stop breathing.  
 
Impact is bigger if they just say it  
without me calculating.  
 
I have a short circuit in my heart.  
 
Brain starts imagining my brother being that age.  
Forcing my neurons to form the image of him.  
 
Flickering some unreal photos of me still being younger than him  
even though I can’t project that character.  
 
I check the wrinkles around their eyes.  
Analyze their every move, gesture, smile, height.  
 
Haircut.  
Grey hairs.  
 
I wonder about his looks.  
 
Their matured expressions, color of their voice, huskiness,  
would he sound like them, I try to hear him.  
 
Even when I was 16, I observed people who were 20,  
looking for him.  
 
Forty, he would have been forty this year.  
Wishful thinking doesn’t stop.  
 
It’s there, all the time, in the back of my head.  
 
What would be his job, would he be married, kids...  
I can’t escape those thoughts, it’s stronger than me.  
 
Seemingly just a number.  
 
But not any number.  
 
Not to me.  
 
‘79.  
~    

_Džemil Hodžić, september 2019_
